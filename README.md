# ⚡ MerdSuite ERP

A modern Electrical Project Management ERP built with React and Vite.

Designed for electricians, electrical contractors and small engineering companies to manage projects, quotations, invoices, inventory and expenses from one dashboard.

---

# Features

## Dashboard
- Business overview
- Revenue summary
- Active projects
- Outstanding invoices

## Clients
- Add clients
- Edit clients
- Delete clients
- Client history

## Projects
- Project management
- Status tracking
- Budget tracking
- Project locations

## Quotations
- Material calculator
- Labour costs
- Transport costs
- Discounts
- Automatic totals
- Save quotations

## Invoices
- Create invoices
- Convert quotation to invoice
- Payment tracking
- Balance calculation

## Inventory
- Material stock
- Cable management
- Purchase records

## Expenses
- Record expenses
- Categorize expenses
- Monthly summaries

## Reports
- Revenue reports
- Expense reports
- Profit reports
